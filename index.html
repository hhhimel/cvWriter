Template

Every template below is single-column and ATS-friendly. Picking one sets a sensible section order, heading wording, and accent colour to start from — all three stay fully editable afterward.

The Europass-style option is an original layout inspired by the Europass CV's conventions — it isn't the official Europass template or generator, and this site isn't affiliated with Europass or the European Union.
